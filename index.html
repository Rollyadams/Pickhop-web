About us

Pickhop is part of Rollyadams Techworld Limited family. We offer a wide range of app-based services within Lagos and across Nigeria.

PICKHOP, our ride-hailing platform, gives car owners, drivers and delivery partners real opportunities to find work and earn. We are open to working with businesses, partners, the taxi and automobile industries, government bodies and communities, today and in the future, to close transportation gaps and tackle unemployment across Nigeria.

PICKHOP is here to improve how Nigerians get around every day. We are building a safe, inclusive and sustainable transportation platform that creates greater social value for local services, cities and the future of Nigeria.

Our mission

Mission

  • To improve the quality of transportation in Nigeria
  • To equip car owners and driver with the opportunity to earn profitable income and still maintain a standard livelihood.
  • To create a safe transport environment for commuters day and night.

Vision

  • To become Nigeria’s most trusted ride-hailing platform
  • To be where drivers earn fairly and keep what they earn
  • To make every trip safe and stress-free
