Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Rollyadams Techworld Limited (“Pickhop”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, shares and protects your personal data when you visit pickhop.com.ng or use the Pickhop app as a rider or a driver. We handle personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).

Summary: we collect only what we need to run rides safely, we verify drivers and riders with their NIN and a face check, we track location only during the periods explained below, we do not sell your personal data, and you can contact us at any time to access, correct or delete your data.

1. Who we are

Pickhop is a ride-hailing platform operated by Rollyadams Techworld Limited, a company registered in Nigeria. For privacy questions, see the Data Protection contact in section 18.

2. Data we collect

Everyone (website and app)

  • Device and technical data: IP address, device name, brand and model, operating system, mobile carrier, network type and time zone, plus basic usage logs such as access times, features used and crashes.
  • Messages and uploads: in-app messages, messages to our support team, and any photos or documents you send with a complaint or report.
  • Details you send us when you contact us, such as your name, email address or phone number.

Riders

  • Account details: name, phone number and email address.
  • Identity verification data: your National Identification Number (NIN), a live face image and the results of face matching and liveness checks (see section 4).
  • Trip data: pick-up and drop-off locations, route, trip time, fare, trip code, and ratings or reports.
  • Emergency contacts you choose to add.

Drivers

  • Account details: full name, phone number and email address.
  • Personal details: profile picture, date of birth, gender, home address and proof of residency.
  • Identity verification data: your National Identification Number (NIN), a live face image and the results of face matching and liveness checks (see section 4), and an official government ID.
  • Licence and eligibility data: driver’s licence data and status, driving record, and relevant insurance.
  • Vehicle information: vehicle type, make, model, year, colour, registration papers, plate number and vehicle photos.
  • Tax identification number and bank details.
  • Where permitted or required by law, background checks and criminal-record information. These checks may be carried out for us by approved vendors.
  • Trip and earnings data: trips, daily fee, VAT collected, acceptance and completion rates, and reports.
  • Payment records: top-up amounts, dates, references and the payment method used (see section 6).
  • Live location while you are online and during trips.

If you do not provide the driver information above, you will not be able to register as a driver.

Incident and complaint data

When you report an accident, injury, lost item or complaint, we may collect trip details, your description of what happened, photos or documents you upload, and your contact details, so that we can review the report and respond.

3. Data we get from other sources

We may also receive information about you from:

  • Identity verification and background-check providers: results of NIN, document and face checks, and, where permitted or required by law, background checks.
  • Payment providers: confirmation that a payment was made, its amount, reference and status.
  • Authorities: law enforcement agencies and other government bodies, where the law requires or allows it.
  • Other users: for example ratings, reviews, reports and complaints about you, and a referral if someone invited you to join.

4. Identity verification (drivers and riders)

To keep everyone safe, every driver and every rider must verify their identity before using Pickhop. This includes:

  • NIN verification: we check your NIN and the details linked to it through approved identity verification providers.
  • Facial recognition: we compare a live image of your face with the image linked to your identity record to confirm that you are the person you claim to be.
  • Liveness check: we check that a real person is present in front of the camera, so that photos, videos or masks cannot be used to impersonate someone.

Face images and face-matching results are biometric data, which the NDPA treats as sensitive personal data. We ask for your explicit consent before collecting them, and we use them only to verify your identity, prevent fraud and protect riders and drivers. We do not use them for advertising. If you do not consent, we cannot activate your account, because verification is required for the safety of riders and drivers.

Other users can see that you are verified, but they never see your NIN or your face data.

5. Location data

We use your device location to match riders and drivers, show pick-up points, calculate fares and run safety features.

When tracking starts and stops

  • Drivers: tracking starts when you go online. It continues while you are online and during trips, and stops when you go offline.
  • Riders: your location is used when you set a pick-up point or request a ride, and tracked during an active trip. Tracking stops when the trip ends.

Location may be collected while the app is in the background, for example while a driver is online or while a trip is active.

Riders can type their pick-up address instead of using their device location. Trip locations are recorded for safety, so that a trip can be reviewed if there is an incident, a complaint or an emergency.

We use location because it is needed to provide rides and keep people safe, and we also ask for your consent. You can turn off location permission in your phone settings at any time, but the app will not work properly without it.

How long we keep location history

We keep trip location history only for as long as it is needed for safety reviews, complaints and disputes, fraud prevention, and legal or tax obligations. After that, we delete it or anonymise it so that it can no longer identify you. Live location while a driver is online but not on a trip is used for matching and is not kept longer than needed for that purpose.

6. Payments

Riders do not pay Pickhop through the app. Riders pay their driver directly for each trip. A rider who pays by bank transfer can see the driver’s bank account details for that trip.

Only drivers pay through the app, to top up the balance used for the daily fee. Drivers can top up by card, bank transfer (NIP), Okra or OPay. These payments are processed by our third-party payment providers, not by Pickhop itself. The providers collect and handle your card or bank details under their own security rules and privacy policies. Pickhop does not store your full card number or card security code.

Pickhop keeps a record of each payment: the amount, date, reference number, payment method and status. We keep these records for accounting, tax, dispute and fraud-prevention purposes.

7. How we use your data, and why

For each purpose, the legal basis we rely on is shown in brackets.

  • To create and manage your account, and to verify drivers and riders. (Contract and legal obligation. Explicit consent for face and other biometric data.)
  • To connect riders with drivers, calculate fares and complete trips. (Contract.)
  • To use your location for matching, trip tracking and safety features such as the trip code check, live trip sharing, SOS and emergency contacts. (Contract and safety, and your consent.)
  • To calculate driver daily fees, VAT and earnings records, and to process driver top-ups. (Contract and legal obligation.)
  • To provide support, handle complaints and investigate incidents. (Contract and legitimate interest.)
  • To detect and prevent fraud and misuse, and to keep our service secure. (Legitimate interest and legal obligation.)
  • To test, analyse and improve our service. (Legitimate interest.)
  • To send you promotions and marketing messages, and to run promotions. (Your consent.)
  • To meet legal, tax and regulatory obligations, and to respond to lawful requests. (Legal obligation.)
  • To protect the rights, safety and property of Pickhop, our users and the public. (Legitimate interest and vital interests.)

8. Automated decisions

Pickhop uses automated systems that can make decisions about accounts without a person reviewing each one first. For example, these systems can:

  • match riders with drivers, based on factors such as availability and distance;
  • recommend fares, including automatic surge pricing;
  • set user ratings and deactivate users with very low ratings;
  • flag or suspend users who appear to be involved in fraud or unsafe or harmful behaviour, and restrict what a driver can do, such as going online, accepting trips or accessing Comfort rides; and
  • check uploaded documents, your NIN and your face match, and decline them automatically if they do not pass. You can resubmit them or ask for a person to review the decision.

Where a decision significantly affects you, you can ask us to have a person review it, tell us your side, and challenge the decision. Contact us using section 18.

9. Marketing, referrals and promotions

  • Marketing messages: we send promotions and marketing messages only where you have agreed to receive them. You can switch off push notifications in your phone or app settings at any time. You can unsubscribe from marketing messages using the link or instructions in them, or by contacting us.
  • Referrals: if you invite a friend and earn a reward such as fee-days, we use the referral record only to check that the conditions were met. The person who invited you does not see any information about you, and you do not see any information about the people you invite.
  • Promotions: to run promotions, bonuses and campaigns, we may use your name, phone number, user ID, activity on Pickhop and payment details, so that we can confirm you qualify and give you the benefit.

10. Who we share data with

What riders and drivers see about each other

  • Drivers see about a rider: name, photo, rating, number of orders, trip details, offered fare and contact details.
  • Riders see about a driver: name, photo, vehicle make, model, plate and colour, live location, ratings and contact details.
  • Verification: each side can see that the other is verified, but never their NIN or face data.
  • Bank details: a rider who pays by bank transfer can see the driver’s bank account details for that trip.

Others we share data with

  • Emergency contacts and people you share a trip with: when you use SOS or live trip sharing, the people you choose can see your trip details.
  • Identity verification and background-check providers: to check NIN details, run face-matching and liveness checks, and, where permitted or required by law, background checks for drivers.
  • Payment providers: card, bank transfer (NIP), Okra and OPay providers that process driver payments.
  • Other service providers: cloud hosting, database and communication providers that help us run Pickhop. They may process data only on our instructions.
  • The Rollyadams Techworld group: other companies in our group, where needed to run and improve our services.
  • Business transfers: a new owner or partner, if Pickhop or part of it is sold, merged or restructured. They must continue to protect your data.
  • Authorities: law enforcement, regulators or courts, where the law requires it or where it is needed to protect safety.
  • Anonymised data: we may share aggregated or anonymised information that can no longer identify you.
  • With your consent: for other purposes, where you agree or ask us to.

We do not sell your personal data.

11. Transfers outside Nigeria

Some of our service providers may store or process data outside Nigeria. Where this happens, we take steps to make sure your data stays protected as the NDPA requires.

12. How long we keep data

We keep your personal data for as long as you have an account with us. After that, we may keep some data for these periods:

  • Tax and payment records: 7 years.
  • Business correspondence: 5 years.
  • Data related to legal claims or disputes: 3 to 5 years, or until the claim is settled, if longer.
  • Fraud and safety data: for as long as needed to prevent fraud, keep users safe and meet our legal obligations.

Where the law requires us to keep data for longer, we keep it for that period. When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it. You can ask us which period applies to your data by contacting us.

13. Security

We protect your data with these measures:

  • encryption of data in transit, using secure connections;
  • encryption of stored sensitive data, such as identity verification data;
  • role-based access, so staff can see only the data their job needs; and
  • regular security reviews.

No system is completely secure, so please keep your account details private.

14. Your rights and choices

Under the NDPA you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • request deletion of your data, where we have no legal reason to keep it;
  • object to or restrict certain processing;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on consent, including consent to biometric verification, location and marketing;
  • ask for human review of an automated decision;
  • receive your data in a portable format; and
  • complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

Using the app: you can view and update your details in the app, and you can request deletion of your account in the app. You can also use any of these rights by contacting us using section 18. We may need to verify your identity first.

Account deletion: we may delay deleting your account if there is an open complaint, an unresolved safety incident or an unpaid amount on it. Some records, such as trip, payment, tax and safety records, may be kept where the law requires or where needed to resolve disputes, for the periods in section 12.

If you withdraw consent for biometric verification, we may have to close your account.

15. Children

Pickhop is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

16. Cookies

Our website does not currently use advertising cookies. You can delete cookies and change your cookie settings in your web browser. If we add analytics or similar tools, we will update this policy.

17. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page with a new effective date, and tell you in the app or by email if the changes are significant.
